# Materials and Product Care

## What the magnets are made of
The front of each fridge magnet is acrylic or resin with a neodymium magnet on the back. It is strong enough to hold
2-3 sheets of paper.

## Cleaning
Wipe with a slightly damp soft cloth. Don't use alcohol, cleaning sprays or solvents; they cloud the surface and fade the
print. Don't put magnets in the dishwasher or soak them.

## Preventing fading
Long exposure to direct sunlight fades the print, so place magnets out of direct sun. Hot spots such as the side of a
fridge next to the stove also speed up ageing.

## Safety
The magnets are strong: keep them away from bank cards, pacemakers and mechanical watches. They contain small parts and
are not suitable for children under 3. Do not put them in a microwave or oven.

## Phone case care
Phone cases are soft TPU. Wash with water and a little mild detergent and let them air dry. Clear cases yellow
naturally over time; this is normal ageing of the material, not a defect.
