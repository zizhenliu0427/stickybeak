# Customs, Duty and Taxes

## Are prices tax inclusive
Prices on the site are final item prices and include Australian GST (10%); no tax is added at checkout. Shipping is
charged separately, see "Shipping and Delivery".

## Import tax for parcels to China
Parcels to mainland China enter as personal items. Chinese customs may charge postal import tax on some parcels;
whether tax applies and how much is decided by customs based on the declared value and product type. Any tax is paid by
the recipient, who is notified directly by customs or the postal service.

## Declared value
We declare the amount actually paid for the order. We can't under-declare or change the item description.

## Parcels held or returned by customs
If customs asks for more information (such as ID details), please follow their notice. If a parcel is returned because
the recipient refused to pay tax or didn't complete clearance, the return shipping and original shipping are not
refunded; the item price is refunded after we receive the returned parcel.
