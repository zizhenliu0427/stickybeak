# Shipping and Delivery

## Where and when we ship
Everything ships from Sydney within 2 business days after payment (later around Australian public holidays). When the
order ships its status changes to "shipped" and the tracking number is emailed to you.

## Shipping within Australia
- Standard (Australia Post): 3-7 business days, AUD 6.95; free for orders of AUD 60 or more.
- Express Post: 1-3 business days, AUD 12.95.
Remote areas may take 1-3 extra business days.

## Shipping to mainland China
We ship to mainland China by tracked international post, usually 10-20 business days, AUD 19.95; free for orders of
AUD 150 or more. Customs clearance time is not included and peak seasons (Singles' Day, around Chinese New Year) can be
slower. See "Customs, Duty and Taxes" for import taxes.

## Where we ship to
We currently ship to Australia, New Zealand and mainland China only. New Zealand shipping is AUD 14.95 and usually takes
6-12 business days. Other countries and regions are not supported yet.

## Tracking not updating or parcel late
Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after dispatch. If your parcel is more than 5 business days later than the
estimate, contact support@stickybeak.com.au with your order number and we will investigate with the carrier. Parcels
confirmed lost are replaced or fully refunded.
