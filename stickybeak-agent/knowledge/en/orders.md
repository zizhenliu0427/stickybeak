# Orders: Status, Tracking and Cancellation

## Order statuses
- pending: placed but not paid; closes automatically if unpaid for 30 minutes
- paid: payment received, waiting to ship
- processing: being packed
- shipped: handed to the carrier; tracking number emailed
- completed: delivered
- cancelled: cancelled before payment, or closed after the payment window expired
- refunding / refunded: refund in progress / returned to the original payment method

## How to cancel an order
Only pending (unpaid) orders can be cancelled by you: click cancel in "My orders", or ask the shopping assistant (you
will be asked to confirm). The stock is released immediately. Paid orders can't be cancelled by you, see
"Returns and Refunds".

## Why was my order closed automatically
We hold the stock for 30 minutes after you place an order. If payment isn't completed in that time the order closes and
the stock is released to other customers. Just place a new order if you still want the items.

## Changing the shipping address
Addresses can't be changed on the website after payment. If the order hasn't shipped, contact
support@stickybeak.com.au with your order number and the new address as soon as possible. Shipped orders can't be changed.

## Checking your orders
Log in and open "My orders" to see every order and its status, or ask the shopping assistant "where is my order". For
privacy you can only look up orders in your own account.
