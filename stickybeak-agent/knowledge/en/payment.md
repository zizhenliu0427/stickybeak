# Payment Methods and Currency

## Accepted payment methods
We accept cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Alipay and WeChat Pay, all through Stripe's secure checkout. We
never store your card details.

## Paying in Chinese yuan
Yes. At checkout you can pay in Australian dollars (AUD) or Chinese yuan (CNY). CNY amounts use the exchange rate at the
time of payment; the rate is saved with the order, so later rate changes don't affect that order or its refund.

## Payment failed
If you close the payment page or the payment fails, the order stays "pending" and you can pay again from "My orders".
Unpaid orders close automatically after 30 minutes and the stock is released; after that you need to place a new order.

## Payment completed after the order closed
If a payment arrives after the order has already closed, it is refunded to the original payment method automatically;
you don't need to contact us. Place a new order to buy the items.

## Charged twice
If an order was charged twice, contact support@stickybeak.com.au with the order number and the charges; once confirmed
the extra payment is refunded to the original payment method.
