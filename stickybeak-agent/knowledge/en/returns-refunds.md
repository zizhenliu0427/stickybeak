# Returns and Refunds

## Faulty, damaged or wrong items (report within 7 days)
If an item arrives damaged, has a manufacturing fault, or is not what you ordered, email support@stickybeak.com.au within
7 days of delivery with your order number and photos. Once confirmed we send a free replacement or give a full refund
including shipping, your choice. You don't need to send the faulty item back.

## Change-of-mind returns
You can return items within 14 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging. Return shipping is
paid by the buyer, and we refund the item price (not the original shipping) after we receive and check the return.
Custom items (for example magnets printed with your own name or campus stop) can't be returned for change of mind,
unless they are faulty.

## Exchanges
We don't do direct exchanges. To get a different design, return the item and place a new order. Faulty items are
replaced directly, see "Faulty, damaged or wrong items".

## How long do refunds take
After a refund is approved the order shows "refunding", then "refunded" once the payment provider confirms it.
Refunds go back to the original payment method: cards usually take 5-10 business days, Alipay and WeChat Pay usually
1-3 business days, depending on the provider. Orders paid in CNY are refunded the CNY amount you paid, regardless of
later exchange-rate changes.

## Cancelling a paid order
Paid orders can't be cancelled on the website. If it hasn't shipped yet, email support@stickybeak.com.au as soon as
possible and our team will refund it. Orders that have already shipped follow the returns process after delivery.
