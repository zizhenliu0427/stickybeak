# Our Story

## The name StickyBeak
"Stickybeak" is Australian slang for a nosy, curious person. We want every little thing we make to make people lean in
for a closer look and remember a piece of life in Australia.

## How we started
StickyBeak was started by a few international students studying in Sydney. Before heading home after graduation we
wanted to take the bus stops and train lines of our daily commute with us, so we turned the stop signs near UNSW, USYD,
UQ, ANU and other campuses into fridge magnets. Our classmates kept asking for them, and the shop began.

## Who designs the products
Every design is original and made by our team in Sydney, inspired by campus bus stops, supermarkets, trains, native
birds and everyday Aussie life. Each series is made in small batches and may not be restocked once sold out.

## Custom orders
We make custom magnets (your campus stop, a name and so on) with a minimum of 20 pieces and about 3 weeks lead time.
